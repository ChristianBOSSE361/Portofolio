## Machine learning and deep learning project
All of these projects were developed not just to apply AI and ML models, but out of a deep desire to understand how they truly work and the fundamental concepts behind different algorithms.

- [**Iris Classification**](https://github.com/ChristianBOSSE361/Artificial_Intelligence)  
  *Tags:* `PyTorch`, `OpenCV`  
  Classify iris flowers based on petal and sepal measurements.

- [**House Price Prediction**](https://github.com/ChristianBOSSE361/Artificial_Intelligence)  
  *Tags:* `Scikit-learn`, `FastAPI`  
  Predict real-estate prices using regression models.

- [**Spam Detection**](https://github.com/ChristianBOSSE361/Artificial_Intelligence)  
  *Tags:* `Scikit-learn`, `FastAPI`, `LangChain`, `FAISS`  
  Classify emails/messages as spam or not spam.

- [**Sentiment or Feelings Detection**](https://github.com/ChristianBOSSE361/Artificial_Intelligence)  
  *Tags:* `Scikit-learn`, `FastAPI`  
  Detect sentiment in text (positive or negative).

- [**Customer Classification**](https://github.com/ChristianBOSSE361/Artificial_Intelligence)  
  *Tags:* `Scikit-learn`, `FastAPI`  
  Group customers into categories for business insights.

- [**Image Classification**](https://github.com/ChristianBOSSE361/Artificial_Intelligence)  
  *Tags:* `Scikit-learn`, `FastAPI`  
  Build CNN models to classify images into categories.

- [**Image Denoising**](https://github.com/ChristianBOSSE361/Artificial_Intelligence)  
  *Tags:* `Scikit-learn`, `FastAPI`  
  Remove noise from images using autoencoders.

- [**RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**](https://github.com/ChristianBOSSE361/Artificial_Intelligence)  
  *Tags:* `Scikit-learn`, `FastAPI`  
  Generate a model able to answer questions about a document using RAG, with an interactive interface.
